## Purpose

Test whether `Get In Line` performs Position 29 Commitment more effectively in the completed Transition Arc than the published `All or Nothing` placement, while preserving Position 30 as a separately engineered Threshold.

The active redesign transition is `Wake Up Call` -> `Get In Line`. Position 30 has no assigned song. `Wake Up Call` remains at Position 28 and must not be represented as a Position 30 assumption.

## Architectural Rationale

The redesign tests enacted Commitment rather than absolute Commitment. `Get In Line` may convert the recognition and Choice in `Wake Up Call` into immediate action while leaving more structural room for a distinct Threshold at Position 30. This is an experimental hypothesis, not approved listening evidence.

Changing Position 29 alters the predecessor, candidate pool, and narrative input used by CDE-0030. The published CDE-0030 remains valid historical evidence for the v0.7 path from `All or Nothing`, but it cannot determine Position 30 in this iteration.

## Approval Conditions

1. Compare `Wake Up Call` -> `Get In Line` against the published `Wake Up Call` -> `All or Nothing` transition.
2. Compare `Get In Line` into each revised Position 30 finalist.
3. Determine whether Position 29 performs owned Commitment without repeating Choice or consuming Threshold.
4. Determine whether the selected Position 30 candidate creates a genuine Scene 3 crossing.
5. Promote the redesign only through a new canonical revision after explicit human approval and required TES and TA work.
