## Preserved Decisions

SD-0002-003 through SD-0002-007 remain in force except where the v1.0 eight-song sequence placed `The Truth Is...` directly after `Hurricane`. TA-0021 remains accepted and continues to govern `Hate My Life` to `Hurricane`.

## Design Decision SD-0002-008

**Decision:** Insert `In Ruins` immediately after `Hurricane` and retain `The Truth Is... (I Lied About Everything)` as the Scene 2 closer.

**Engineering Function:** Controlled Descent

**Rationale:**

`Hurricane` must remain after `Hate My Life` because it completes Recovery by establishing resilience and forward motion. `The Truth Is...` must remain the closer because unresolved honesty creates anticipation for Scene 3. Inserting `In Ruins` between them removes the same-album adjacency and provides a deliberate reduction in emotional temperature after forward engagement has already been restored. Direct listening review approved the revised four-song close as a continuous experience.

## Alternatives Considered

- Retaining the original adjacency would preserve compactness but require an explicit same-album exception that lacks transition evidence.
- `Get In Line` restores energy through defiance but is too conclusive for the descent into unresolved honesty.
- `Shape of My Heart` provides empathy but risks making the close overly subdued.
- Moving or removing `Hurricane` would invalidate the accepted TA-0021 transition and reopen Recovery.

## Validation Boundary

This design revision approves sequence architecture. It does not certify the new transitions. Scene 2 remains in progress pending:

- TES-0022 and TA-0022 for `Hurricane` to `In Ruins`;
- TES-0023 and TA-0023 for `In Ruins` to `The Truth Is...`;
- the later Scene 2 to Scene 3 boundary audit.

## Design Lock Statement

SD-0002 v1.1 is Design Locked because the approved insertion preserves all accepted upstream functions, removes the identified album-distribution conflict, keeps the established closer, and passed direct comparative listening review. Any further sequence change requires a new Engineering Change Proposal and design revision.
