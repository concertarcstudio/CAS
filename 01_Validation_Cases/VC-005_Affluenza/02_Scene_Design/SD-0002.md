## Engineering Objective

**Status:** Locked

## Listener Objective

**Status:** Locked

## Scene Thesis

Authenticity builds connection.

**Status:** Locked

## Movement I — Invitation

### Movement Metadata

- **Movement:** I — Invitation
- **Purpose:** Transform curiosity into empathy and emotional investment.
- **Listener State:** Curiosity → Empathy → Hope → Acceptance
- **Entry Song:** `Medusa (Stone)`
- **Status:** Locked

| Position | Song | Engineering Function |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Medusa (Stone) | Invitation — reset expectations and open a darker emotional chapter. |
| 2 | The One | Humanization — move the listener from observation into empathy. |
| 3 | Nothing Could Come Between Us | Hope — create emotional investment by presenting the possibility that the relationship can endure. |
| 4 | Not Meant to Be | Emotional Center — resolve hope into acceptance and establish the scene's emotional truth. |

### Design Decision SD-0002-003

**Decision:** Retain `The One` immediately after `Medusa (Stone)` and lock the four-song opening movement.

**Rationale:**

The movement progressively changes the listener's state from curiosity to empathy, hope, and acceptance. `The One` performs the essential humanization function between the atmospheric opening and the relationship arc. Removing it turns the progression into a sequence of events; retaining it makes the listener participate emotionally.

### Alternatives Considered

- Opening directly with `The One` weakened the distinction from Scene 1.
- Moving `Nothing Could Come Between Us` directly after `Medusa (Stone)` reduced the humanization step.
- Using `Say Goodbye` before `Not Meant to Be` duplicated loss and weakened the emotional center.

## Movement II — Integration

### Movement Metadata

- **Movement:** II — Integration
- **Purpose:** Validate the emotional center and prepare continued engagement.
- **Listener State:** Acceptance → Reflection
- **Entry Song:** `Say Goodbye`
- **Status:** Provisional

### Design Decision SD-0002-004

**Decision:** Retain `Say Goodbye` as the provisional Integration opener immediately after `Not Meant to Be`.

**Rationale:**

`Say Goodbye` acknowledges and extends the emotional truth established by `Not Meant to Be` without forcing an immediate tonal reset. It gives the emotional center room to resonate, converts acceptance into reflection, and creates a controlled bridge toward the recovery movement.

### Alternatives Considered

- `Hate My Life` created immediate relief but risked undercutting the emotional center before it had time to resonate.
- `Hurricane` restored momentum but did not validate the emotional truth as directly.
- Moving directly into a high-energy track weakened continuity and shortened the emotional aftermath.

## Design Lock Criteria

SD-0002 will advance to v1.0 when:

- all movements are sequenced and justified;

- credible alternatives have been tested;
