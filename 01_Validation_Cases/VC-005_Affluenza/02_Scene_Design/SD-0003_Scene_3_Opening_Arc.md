## Authority Finding

Repository authority fixes Scene 3 at global positions 31 through 45 and fixes `PCH` at Position 30 as the accepted Threshold and Scene 3 handoff. No existing record defines a Scene 3 objective, a Position 31 function, or a complete Scene 3 movement architecture. TA-0030 expressly assigns the next engineering function to later Scene 3 design authority.

`Arrival` is therefore not an established Position 31 function and is not adopted by this record.

## Scene 3 Objective

Convert the unresolved forward energy delivered across the Threshold into a distinct new phase of active engagement without returning to Scene 2 reflection, repeating Commitment, or resolving the later concert arc prematurely.

This objective is intentionally bounded. It authorizes opening work without inventing the complete narrative, listener-state sequence, movement map, or closing function for Scene 3.

## Opening Arc Scope

- **Global envelope:** Positions 31 through 34, provisionally reserved for the Scene 3 Opening Arc.
- **Current design lock:** Position 31 only.
- **Positions 32 through 34:** Functions and placements remain undefined pending the human Position 31 decision and boundary listening.
- **Positions 35 through 45:** Outside this record and fully unresolved.

The four-position envelope is a planning boundary, not sequence authority. It may be revised through later Scene 3 design work.

## Position 31 Engineering Function

**Scene Activation:** Position 31 must make the new scene perceptible by converting `PCH`'s directional Threshold into an active present-tense condition. It must preserve emotional continuity, establish forward runway, and avoid functioning as a second Threshold, another Commitment statement, a return to Reflection, or premature resolution.

### Function Test

A candidate performs Scene Activation when comparative listening supports all of the following:

1. `PCH` reads as the completed crossing and the candidate reads as activity on the other side.
2. The boundary is perceptible without an abrupt emotional reset.
3. The candidate opens usable narrative and musical runway for later Scene 3 positions.
4. The candidate does not merely rename movement as `Arrival` or repeat Threshold.

## Boundary Validation Requirement

The deferred Scene 2 to Scene 3 boundary audit now attaches to `PCH` to the eventual Position 31 selection. Human comparative listening must determine whether `PCH` completes the crossing and the selected candidate activates a distinct new scene while preserving continuity and forward runway.

No boundary approval, canonical placement, TES-0031, or TA-0031 is authorized by this design record.
