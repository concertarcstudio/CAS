## Comparative Listening Package

Listen to the same boundary window for each finalist, including enough of the outgoing ending and incoming opening to judge the complete handoff.

1. `All or Nothing` -> `PCH`: Does absolute Commitment become unmistakable forward passage, and does the incoming opening leave a useful unresolved door into Scene 3 rather than merely changing scenery?
2. `All or Nothing` -> `Get In Line`: Does Commitment turn into an active crossing, or does the incoming stance read mainly as compliance or defiance and repeat the prior function?
3. `All or Nothing` -> `Head Above Water`: Does emotional survival carry the listener across the Scene 2 boundary, or does it prolong the existing struggle and delay the threshold?

For all three, compare boundary clarity, forward motion, emotional temperature, continuity from the outgoing ending, absence of false closure, and the degree to which Scene 3 remains open but not prematurely begun.
