## Decision Context

- **Engineering Function:** Threshold. Position 30 must carry the enacted Commitment established by `Get In Line` across the Scene 2 boundary, create unmistakable forward passage, and hand unresolved narrative energy to Scene 3 without repeating defiance or beginning the next scene prematurely.
- **Scene Objective:** Conclude the six-position Transition Arc as a passage: honesty response, consequence, reflection, choice, enacted commitment, then threshold.
- **Experimental Sequence:** Position 25 `History of Violence`; Position 26 `Does It Really Matter`; Position 27 `Shape of My Heart`; Position 28 `Wake Up Call`; Position 29 `Get In Line`; Position 30 unresolved.
- **Design Constraints:** Preserve main and canonical v0.7; use `Get In Line` as the experimental predecessor; do not reuse a placed song; do not draw from Archive positions 59 through 78; avoid known same-album adjacency; distinguish Threshold from the action and defiance already supplied by Position 29; avoid final closure; do not claim listening evidence absent from the repository.
- **Invalidated assumption:** CDE-0030 used `All or Nothing` as predecessor. Its scores and shortlist do not transfer to this sequence.
- **Candidate Pool Source:** The revised pool contains the 29 remaining unplaced baseline songs after placing `Get In Line` at Position 29 and returning `All or Nothing` to the eligible pool.

## Recommendation

- **Recommended candidate:** `PCH`
- **Confidence:** Medium
- **Basis:** `PCH` remains the strongest desk-analysis Threshold because it creates directional passage, preserves an open Scene 3 handoff, and most clearly avoids repeating `Get In Line`'s action or defiance function. It leads the revised model by 0.6 points.
- **Change from CDE-0030:** `Get In Line` is no longer eligible because it occupies experimental Position 29. `Sideways` enters the Top 3. `Head Above Water` moves to second. `All or Nothing` returns to the pool but ranks below the shortlist because it risks repeating Commitment one position later.
- **Unresolved uncertainty:** No candidate-level audio, edition, duration, availability, lyrical, or transition manifest exists. All scoring remains desk analysis.
- **Required human action:** Conduct fresh comparative listening. The original transitions from `All or Nothing` are not valid substitutes.

## Comparative Listening Package

1. `Get In Line` -> `PCH`: Does enacted Commitment become a distinct directional crossing, and does Scene 3 remain open?
2. `Get In Line` -> `Head Above Water`: Does the emotional shift create a threshold, or does it return the arc to unresolved struggle?

Also compare the complete four-song window `Shape of My Heart` -> `Wake Up Call` -> `Get In Line` -> each finalist against the published v0.7 path. Record boundary clarity, function separation, emotional temperature, forward motion, false closure risk, and Scene 3 readiness.
