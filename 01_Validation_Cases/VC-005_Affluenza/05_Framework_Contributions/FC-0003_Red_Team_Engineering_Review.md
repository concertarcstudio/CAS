# FC-0003 — Red Team Engineering Review

## Status

Candidate — validated in Scene 1

## Source

VC-005 / ES-0001

## Problem

Engineering teams can become attached to the current solution. Without a structured challenge, a transition that works well can be mistaken for the strongest available solution.

## Proposal

Before finalizing a significant engineering decision:

1. State the preferred solution.
2. Identify at least one credible alternative when one exists.
3. Evaluate the alternative objectively.
4. Retain the preferred solution only if it remains stronger, or adopt the alternative when it performs better.
5. Record the rationale.

## Expected Benefits

- Stronger decisions.
- Reduced confirmation bias.
- More defensible certification records.
- Better traceability of rejected alternatives.

## Validation Plan

Apply Red Team review through the remainder of VC-005 and evaluate whether it improves decisions without creating unnecessary process.
