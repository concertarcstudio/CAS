# FC-0001 — Identity Expansion

## Status

Candidate — validated in Scene 1

## Source

VC-005 / ES-0001 / TA-0003

## Proposal

Add Identity Expansion as a candidate transition-analysis dimension measuring how effectively a transition reveals a new and meaningful dimension of the artist without disrupting narrative cohesion.

## Definition

Identity Expansion measures how effectively a transition reveals a new and meaningful dimension of the artist without disrupting narrative cohesion.

## Problem Addressed

Traditional transition scoring can overemphasize energy continuity and overlook transitions whose main value is broadening the listener's understanding of the artist.

## Evidence

TA-0003 (`Bad Girlfriend` → `Blow`) demonstrated that a transition can preserve momentum while changing the type of energy and revealing a heavier dimension of the artist.

## Recommendation

Evaluate Identity Expansion throughout VC-005. Consider adoption into CAS-T-203 and CAS-T-206 only after additional validation.
