## 2. Audit Objective

### Intent

Determine whether the reviewed evidence in TES-0021 supports retaining the canonical transition as a controlled escalation from shared frustration and relief through recognition into resilience and forward motion within Recovery.

## 3. Scope of Validation

This audit evaluates only `Hate My Life` to `Hurricane`. It does not certify Movement III or Scene 2 as a whole. TES-0021 is the exclusive evidence source. SD-0002 and the canonical sequence supply design intent and sequence authority, not evidence.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

`Hurricane` should concentrate the emotional pressure established by `Hate My Life`, increase intensity without introducing a disconnected event, and convert relief through recognition into resilience and forward motion without narrative regression, musical disruption, or structural drift.

## 5. Evidence Summary

TES-0021 contains five reviewed observations. All five support the design intent, none contradict it, and average confidence is High.

- OBS-0021-001 supports narrative continuation through intensified existing pressure.
- OBS-0021-002 supports earned emotional escalation from broad frustration into immersive crisis.
- OBS-0021-003 supports increased musical intensity without disruption or fatigue.
- OBS-0021-004 supports deepening Recovery without premature resolution or movement change.
- OBS-0021-005 supports the listener-state change from relief through recognition to resilience and forward motion.

## 9. Engineering Findings

1. Narrative continuity is preserved as `Hurricane` intensifies the struggle already established by `Hate My Life`.
2. Broad frustration develops into an immersive emotional crisis without an unrelated emotional reset.
3. Increased musical scale reinforces escalation and forward momentum without stylistic disruption or listener fatigue.
4. The transition deepens Movement III without prematurely resolving Recovery or signaling a new movement.
5. The listener state progresses from relief through recognition to resilience and forward motion while emotional pressure remains continuous.
6. EP-0001 and EP-0002 found no regression or evaluated failure, but both remain experimental trials.
7. No reviewed evidence supports resequencing, rejection, or a noncanonical direct transition.
