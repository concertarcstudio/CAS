### Transition

`Medusa (Stone)` to `The One`

## 2. Audit Objective

### Intent

Determine whether the reviewed evidence in TES-0016 supports retaining the transition from `Medusa (Stone)` to `The One` as the movement from curiosity into empathy while preserving intrigue and the darker emotional atmosphere established by the Scene 2 opener.

## 3. Scope of Validation

This audit evaluates only the transition between `Medusa (Stone)` and `The One` within Scene 2, Movement I. Its conclusions do not certify the movement or scene as a whole. Broader conclusions remain subject to subsequent Transition Audits, SA-0002, and SC-0002.

TES-0016 is the exclusive evidence source for this audit. SD-0002 supplies the design intent under evaluation but is not treated as evidence.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

The transition should move the listener from curiosity toward empathy. `The One` should reduce emotional distance from the narrator while retaining enough intrigue, musical continuity, and structural discipline to keep the listener oriented within Movement I.

## 5. Evidence Summary

TES-0016 contains five reviewed observations. All five support the design intent, none contradict it, and the reported average confidence is High.

- OBS-0016-001 supports narrative accessibility without loss of intrigue.
- OBS-0016-002 supports reduced emotional distance and increased identification.
- OBS-0016-003 supports continuity of energy, production, and pacing.
- OBS-0016-004 supports structural containment within Movement I.
- OBS-0016-005 supports empathy becoming dominant while curiosity remains present.

This summary identifies the evidentiary function of each observation without reproducing its raw statement.

## 7. Contradicting Evidence

TES-0016 records no contradicting observations. This absence supports acceptance at the transition level but does not resolve the evidence gaps identified in TES-0016.

## 8. Engineering Findings

1. The reviewed evidence supports the intended change from curiosity toward empathy.
2. The transition preserves intrigue while making the narrator more emotionally accessible.
3. Musical continuity maintains listener orientation through the handoff.
4. The transition remains within the structural role assigned to Movement I.
5. No reviewed observation in TES-0016 provides a basis for resequencing or rejection.
6. These findings apply only to this transition and do not establish scene-level coherence or certification.

## 10. Engineering Decision

### Decision

**Accepted:** retain `Medusa (Stone)` to `The One` in its current sequence.

TES-0016 provides complete transition-level evidence for the stated objective: five reviewed observations support the design intent, no contradicting observations are recorded, and the remaining gaps concern downstream persistence, listener generalization, and movement-level coherence. Acceptance is therefore limited to this transition and does not constitute scene certification.

## 12. Lessons for CAS

- A Transition Audit can remain concise when it references stable observation IDs instead of restating evidence.
- Acceptance should be bounded to the level directly supported by the evidence.
- Evidence gaps can remain open without blocking a transition decision when they concern downstream validation and are assigned to named records or activities.
- Explicit separation of design intent, evidence, findings, and decision makes the audit chain reviewable and reusable.
