## 2. Audit Objective

### Intent

Determine whether TES-0026 supports selecting `Does It Really Matter` at Position 26 as the Consequence function and whether the human override of the CDE recommendation is evidence-based and traceable.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

`Does It Really Matter` should advance the architecture after `History of Violence`, preserve continuity, make Consequence legible, and avoid the reduced progression or sequence repetition observed in the alternatives.

## 5. Evidence Summary

TES-0026 contains five reviewed observations. All five support the selected transition. It also preserves the CDE ranking of `Echoes` as contradicting desk-analysis evidence.

- OBS-0026-001 supports the strongest architectural progression with continuity.
- OBS-0026-002 identifies excessive stylistic similarity in `Echoes`.
- OBS-0026-003 identifies same-album adjacency in `World Keeps Spinning`.
- OBS-0026-004 supports the Consequence narrative function.
- OBS-0026-005 supports forward listener-state movement through useful contrast.

## 7. Contradicting Evidence

The CDE recommendation favored `Echoes` with a score of 93.5, compared with 89.3 for `Does It Really Matter`. TES-0026 preserves this result and explains the override. The score was based on desk analysis, while the reviewed human evidence directly evaluates stylistic similarity, album adjacency, and perceived architectural progression in the transition.

## 8. Engineering Findings

1. `Does It Really Matter` provides the strongest reviewed architectural progression after `History of Violence` while maintaining continuity.
2. `Echoes` is not selected because stylistic similarity to the predecessor reduces perceived progression.
3. `World Keeps Spinning` is not selected because same-album adjacency creates avoidable sequence repetition.
4. The selected transition performs Consequence by questioning the meaning and effect of the preceding harm without resolving the larger narrative.
5. The human override is traceable to reviewed comparative evidence and does not erase the original CDE recommendation.
