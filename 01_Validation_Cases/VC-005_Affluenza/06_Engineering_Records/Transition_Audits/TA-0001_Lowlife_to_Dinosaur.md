# TA-0001 — Lowlife → Dinosaur

## Transition

`Lowlife` → `Dinosaur`

## Intent

Confirm the opener's identity statement and convert immediate attitude into forward momentum without spending the scene's full energy reserve.

## Status

Accepted

## Record Control

- **Validation Case:** VC-005
- **Scene:** Scene 1 — Ignition
- **Positions:** 1 → 2
- **Audit Stage:** Narrative Build

## Transition Function

Establish / confirm identity.

## Engineering Assessment

### Narrative

`Lowlife` establishes the band's irreverent, self-aware identity. `Dinosaur` follows by widening that personality into a contemporary generational statement. The second track confirms that the opener was not an isolated novelty; the performance has a sustained point of view.

### Energy

Both tracks carry high engagement, but the transition does not feel like a duplicated peak. `Lowlife` acts as the entrance and identity declaration, while `Dinosaur` functions as momentum confirmation. The sequence remains elevated without exhausting the act's larger payoffs.

### Era and Album Distribution

The transition moves from *The Truth Is... (Special Edition)* to *Dinosaur*, immediately demonstrating cross-era range and avoiding an opening album block.

### Risk

The pair is strongly personality-driven. A third consecutive song with the same comic or defiant function would risk narrowing the opening. Track 3 should therefore change the type of payoff even if it preserves intensity.
