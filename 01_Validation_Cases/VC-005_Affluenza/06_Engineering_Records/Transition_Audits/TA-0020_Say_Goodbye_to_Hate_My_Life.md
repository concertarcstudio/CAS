## 2. Audit Objective

### Intent

Determine whether the reviewed evidence in TES-0020 supports retaining the canonical transition as a controlled broadening from private reflection into shared frustration that restores energy and establishes Recovery.

## 3. Scope of Validation

This audit evaluates only `Say Goodbye` to `Hate My Life`. It does not certify either movement or Scene 2 as a whole. TES-0020 is the exclusive evidence source. SD-0002 and the canonical sequence supply design intent and sequence authority, not evidence.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

`Hate My Life` should preserve the emotional truth established by `Say Goodbye`, broaden specific relationship loss into relatable life frustration, and move the listener from reflection to relief through recognition without narrative discontinuity, musical disruption, or premature resolution.

## 5. Evidence Summary

TES-0020 contains five reviewed observations. All five support the design intent, none contradict it, and average confidence is High.

- OBS-0020-001 supports narrative broadening without beginning an unrelated story.
- OBS-0020-002 supports emotional continuity as resignation becomes outward frustration.
- OBS-0020-003 supports an energy increase experienced as escalation rather than disruption.
- OBS-0020-004 supports the canonical Movement II to III boundary function.
- OBS-0020-005 supports the listener-state change from reflection to relief through recognition.

## 7. Experimental Trial Review

### EP-0001 - Experimental Trial

The continuity trial observed the expected input state of reflection and output state of relief through recognition. No regression was detected. The trial improved traceability and audit clarity but remains experimental and is not a framework requirement.

### EP-0002 - Experimental Trial

The stress trial evaluated emotional leap, musical disruption, narrative regression, and structural drift. No failure was observed. Residual transition-level risk is Low. The trial remains experimental and is not a framework requirement.

## 8. Contradicting Evidence

TES-0020 records no contradicting observation. The plausible tonal-whiplash risk is addressed by reviewed evidence showing that shared frustration preserves unresolved emotional pressure while restoring energy. Absence of contradiction does not close the deferred evidence gaps.

## 9. Engineering Findings

1. Narrative continuity is preserved as the perspective widens from a specific goodbye to generalized dissatisfaction.
2. Resignation and reflection develop into outward frustration without an unrelated emotional reset.
3. The energy increase restores momentum and reads as escalation rather than musical disruption.
4. The transition performs the locked boundary role between Integration and Recovery.
5. The listener state progresses from reflection to relief through recognition while emotional investment remains intact.
6. EP-0001 and EP-0002 found no regression or evaluated failure, but both remain experimental trials.
7. No reviewed evidence supports resequencing, rejection, or a noncanonical direct transition.
