## 2. Audit Objective

### Intent

Determine whether the reviewed evidence in TES-0022 supports retaining the canonical transition as a controlled descent from resilience and forward motion into darker introspection without loss of continuity or listener engagement.

## 3. Scope of Validation

This audit evaluates only `Hurricane` to `In Ruins`. It does not certify Movement IV or Scene 2 as a whole. TES-0022 is the exclusive evidence source. ECP-0001, SD-0002 v1.1, and canonical sequence v0.3 supply change authority, design intent, and sequence authority, not evidence.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

`In Ruins` should reduce emotional and musical intensity after the resilience established by `Hurricane`, create engaged introspection rather than collapse or disengagement, and open Movement IV without narrative discontinuity or structural drift.

## 5. Evidence Summary

TES-0022 contains five reviewed observations. All five support the design intent, none contradict it, and average confidence is High.

- OBS-0022-001 supports narrative continuation through emotional aftermath and inward focus.
- OBS-0022-002 supports a controlled emotional descent without abrupt collapse.
- OBS-0022-003 supports intentional musical reduction without disruption or lost attention.
- OBS-0022-004 supports the boundary from Recovery into Transition without premature confession.
- OBS-0022-005 supports the listener-state change from resilience into engaged introspection and quiet deterioration.

## 7. Experimental Trial Review

### EP-0001 - Experimental Trial

The continuity trial observed the expected input state of resilience and forward motion and the output state of engaged introspection and quiet deterioration. No regression was detected. The trial improved traceability and audit clarity but remains experimental and is not a framework requirement.

### EP-0002 - Experimental Trial

The stress trial evaluated emotional collapse, musical disruption, narrative discontinuity, and structural drift. No failure was observed. Residual transition-level risk is Low. The trial remains experimental and is not a framework requirement.

## 8. Contradicting Evidence

TES-0022 records no contradicting observation. The plausible risk that reduced intensity could cause disengagement or make the close overly subdued is addressed at transition level by reviewed evidence showing preserved attention and emotional continuity. Absence of contradiction does not close the deferred evidence gaps.

## 9. Engineering Findings

1. Narrative continuity is preserved because `In Ruins` functions as the aftermath of `Hurricane` rather than an unrelated story.
2. Emotional intensity decreases deliberately from resilience into quiet deterioration without becoming an abrupt collapse.
3. Reduced musical intensity creates processing space without stylistic disruption or loss of forward attention.
4. The transition closes Recovery after resilience is established and opens Movement IV without prematurely delivering the next confession.
5. The listener state progresses into engaged introspection while investment in the same emotional journey remains intact.
6. EP-0001 and EP-0002 found no regression or evaluated failure, but both remain experimental trials.
7. No reviewed evidence supports resequencing, rejection, or a noncanonical direct transition.
