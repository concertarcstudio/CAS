### Intent

Determine whether the reviewed evidence in TES-0023 supports retaining the canonical transition as an earned arrival from controlled descent into unresolved honesty while preserving continuity, listener engagement, and the Scene 2 closing function.

## 4. Engineering Hypothesis

`The Truth Is... (I Lied About Everything)` should emerge naturally from the deterioration established by `In Ruins`, preserve the darker atmosphere, and close Scene 2 through unresolved honesty that maintains forward curiosity rather than delivering emotional resolution.

- OBS-0023-005 supports the listener-state change from controlled descent into unresolved honesty and curiosity.
