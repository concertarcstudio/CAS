The CDE recommended `All or Nothing` at 95.3, ahead of `Wake Up Call` at 91.2. TES-0028 records that comparative listening found the recommendation too absolute and functionally premature because it began Commitment. This contradiction is resolved by the human override and remains visible in CDE-0028 and TES-0028.

## 8. Engineering Findings

1. `Wake Up Call` is the first-ranked human finalist for the transition after `Shape of My Heart`.
2. The human decision selects `Wake Up Call` at Position 28 for Choice.
3. The human decision does not agree with the CDE recommendation.
4. `All or Nothing` is retained as the CDE recommendation and second-ranked human finalist.
5. `Get In Line` is retained as the third-ranked human finalist.
6. The override is justified because `All or Nothing` felt too absolute and began Commitment too early, while `Wake Up Call` preserved progression from Reflection through Choice.
