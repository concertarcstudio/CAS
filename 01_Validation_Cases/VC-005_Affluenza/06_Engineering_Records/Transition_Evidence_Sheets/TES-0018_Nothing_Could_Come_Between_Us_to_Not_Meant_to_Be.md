## Engineering Intent

Evaluate whether the emotional investment established in `Nothing Could Come Between Us` evolves into a believable confrontation with reality while preserving listener engagement and Movement I continuity.

## Engineering Hypothesis

- **Expected Listener-State Change:** Hope to Acceptance
- **Observed Progression Under Review:** Hope to Reflective Vulnerability
- **Prediction:** Hope should be challenged and resolved into reflective vulnerability and acceptance without being erased or causing listener-state regression.

## Design Intent Reference
