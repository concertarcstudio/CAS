## Engineering Intent

Evaluate whether the transition protects the emotional center established by `Not Meant to Be` and converts acceptance into reflection without duplicating loss, interrupting musical continuity, or releasing tension too early.

## Engineering Hypothesis

- **Expected Listener-State Change:** Acceptance to Reflection
- **Prediction:** `Say Goodbye` should extend the emotional truth of `Not Meant to Be` while creating a distinct reflective aftermath and a controlled bridge into Recovery.

## Design Intent Reference

The design intent in [SD-0002](../../02_Scene_Design/SD-0002.md) is the hypothesis under evaluation, not evidence.

> `Say Goodbye` should validate the emotional center, convert acceptance into reflection, and prepare continued engagement.
