# Transition Evidence Sheets (TES)

Transition Evidence Sheets capture raw engineering observations before formal analysis.

## Purpose

A TES is the engineer's working notebook. It records observations without drawing conclusions.

## Evidence Chain

TES → TA → SA → SC

- **TES** — Raw observations and evidence.
- **TA** — Transition Audit with engineering findings and decisions.
- **SA** — Scene Analysis summarizing validated transition results.
- **SC** — Certification record based on validated engineering evidence.

## Principles

- Record observations before conclusions.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Preserve traceability throughout the engineering process.
- Do not treat TES documents as certified engineering records.
