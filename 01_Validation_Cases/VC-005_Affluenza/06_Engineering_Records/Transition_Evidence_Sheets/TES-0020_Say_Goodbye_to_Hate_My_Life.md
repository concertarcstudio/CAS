## Transition

- **From:** `Say Goodbye`
- **To:** `Hate My Life`

## Engineering Intent

Evaluate whether the transition broadens reflection on relationship loss into shared life frustration while preserving emotional truth, restoring energy, and establishing Recovery without narrative discontinuity or tonal whiplash.

## Engineering Hypothesis

- **Expected Listener-State Change:** Reflection to Relief through recognition
- **Prediction:** `Hate My Life` should redirect unresolved emotion outward into relatable frustration, increasing energy without erasing the reflective aftermath established by `Say Goodbye`.

## Design Intent Reference

The [canonical VC-005 sequence](../../00_Playlist/VC-005_Reconstructed_Canonical_Sequence_v0.2.md) establishes `Say Goodbye` at position 20 and `Hate My Life` at position 21.
