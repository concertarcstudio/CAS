# CAS Engineering Charter

## Mission

Design, evaluate, document, and certify narrative music experiences through intentional, evidence-based engineering.

## Principles

1. **Engineering before documentation.** Documentation records engineering; it does not replace it.
2. **Evidence before opinion.** Recommendations must be supported by observable engineering reasoning.
3. **Frameworks evolve through validation.** Framework changes are proposed through Validation Cases and adopted only after review.
4. **The absence of change is a valid engineering outcome.** Retaining a sequence can be the strongest decision.
5. **Certification is earned, not assumed.** Every certification claim must be traceable to evidence.
6. **The repository is the source of truth.** Conversations generate ideas; committed records preserve decisions.
7. **Challenge preferred solutions before certifying them.** Significant decisions should survive a credible Red Team review.

## Engineering Cycle

1. Select an Engineering Work Item.
2. Perform a Transition Audit or other focused analysis.
3. Record the observation.
4. Determine whether a decision or baseline change is required.
5. Create an Engineering Decision or Engineering Change Proposal when warranted.
6. Record supporting evidence.
7. Update the Scene Analysis and Scene Scorecard.
8. Commit the records.
9. Close the engineering session with the next action identified.

## Operating Principle

> Engineering first. Framework second.
